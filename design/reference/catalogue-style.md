# Betagro catalogue visual style (Betagro_Trade_MKT_.pdf, 2026-10-22)

- **Colours:** deep forest green (~#1A4A20 / #20602F) for covers and brand panels; fresh leaf green (~#78B040) for Thai product names, "PACK SIZE" pills and round category badges; orange (#E87A1E) only for processed-food sections. Pages sit on near-white or very pale grey/green paper texture (~#F4F5F1, #E7EFE2).
- **Layout:** A4 portrait, airy grid of 2–3 products per row; pack shot on the left, text block on the right. Big thin all-caps English section title ("PORK", "CHICKEN") with a small green Thai subtitle, a hairline rule running to a round green badge with a white animal icon in the top-right corner.
- **Typography:** light geometric sans (Gotham/Avenir-like) in thin weights and wide letter-spacing for English; rounded modern Thai sans in green, bold-ish, for product names; small grey item code above (e.g. C-BTG-04) and grey English name below.
- **Product block:** code → green Thai name → grey English name → thin underline → green rounded "PACK SIZE :" pill → sizes in grey → tiny frozen/chilled icons.
- **Photo treatment:** packaged product shots, front-on, cut out on the light background with a soft drop shadow under the pack; no frames or borders. Section openers use full-bleed, warm, appetising food photography (raw cuts on wood boards with herbs, chillies, garlic).
- **Feel:** clean, premium, lots of white space; green is the only strong accent, everything else is white/grey.
